# Prompt for Claude Design — Dr. Atlanta brand kit

Paste everything below the line into Claude Design, and attach the contents of this `brand-kit/` folder (at minimum: `logos/`, `colors.json`, `type.md`, `fonts/`, and a handful of frames from `photos/`).

---

Build a complete brand kit for **Dr. Atlanta Cosmetic Dentistry**, the practice of **Dr. Eric Ennuson, DDS** (USC Herman Ostrow; licensed by the Georgia Board of Dentistry), a porcelain-veneer and smile-design practice serving **metro Atlanta**. The brand already exists on the website; your job is to formalize it, extend it to print, social and environment, and hand back editable, production-ready files. Do not redesign the identity — refine and systematize what is attached.

## The identity as it stands

- **Wordmark:** "Dr. Atlanta" in Newsreader (weight 400, tracking −0.015em) ending in a gold period, followed on the same baseline by "Cosmetic Dentistry" in Hanken Grotesk at 53% of the name's size in secondary grey. Transparent PNGs for light and dark backgrounds are attached (`logos/`), plus the HTML they were rendered from. Treat the gold period as the signature: it is the only colour in the mark.
- **Monogram:** the serif capital **A** with the same gold dot (attached), used on an ink square for the app icon and favicon.
- **Palette** (exact values in `colors.json`): porcelain #F2F1ED (page), porcelain-2 #E9E7E1 (surfaces), ink #16181B (dark sections, buttons), text #1E2023, text-2 #5C5F64, mark-gold #B9975B (accent only), gold-text #7F6128 (gold for small type on light). Proportion rule: roughly 70% porcelain, 25% ink, under 2% gold. Gold is punctuation, never a field, never a gradient.
- **Type** (`type.md`): Newsreader for anything with voice — headlines, prices, statements — at weights 300–400 with the italic as the emphasis device; Hanken Grotesk 400/500 for everything functional. Headlines are sentence-case statements that end in a period: "The price is the price." "White is not one color." "Designed for your face, not from a template." No all-caps eyebrows, no letterspaced labels, no bold serif, no 01/02/03 ornaments.
- **Photography:** the practice's own frames (attached): the doctor in burgundy scrubs, loupes, the operatory light, real before/after cases. Daylight, warm, unretouched skin, shallow depth. Never stock smiles, never blue-white "dental" lighting, never clip-art teeth.
- **Feel:** a quiet luxury register with a clinical spine — porcelain and ink, big serif statements, lots of air, one gold accent, cinematic dark hero footage. Closer to an architecture studio or a watchmaker than to a dental chain. Avoid the cosmetic-dentistry clichés: cream-and-gold swirls, sparkles, tooth icons, gradients, teal.

## Voice and legal constraints (non-negotiable)

- Plain, confident, specific. Short declarative sentences. Prices and facts stated outright.
- **No superlatives or comparatives** anywhere — not "best", "#1", "top", "premier", "elite", "painless", "specialist" (Georgia Board of Dentistry advertising rule 150-10-.01(6)). Safe phrasing: "smile design for metro Atlanta", "designed for your face, not from a template", "a licensed dentist, not a veneer tech".
- **Never** use "melanin-rich" or lead with skin tone. The shade story is "matched to you, not a chart" and "white is not one color".
- Price language: "Full-arch smile design, $8,999 — published, confirmed in writing before treatment." Any monthly figure must sit with its terms: "36 payments at 0% APR, $0 down, on the $8,999 full arch. Representative example; subject to credit approval. Financing through Cherry, Sunbit, and HFD."
- Location: say "metro Atlanta" up front; the street address (at BRUSH Dentistry, 1475 Buford Dr, Suite 204, Lawrenceville, GA 30043) belongs only in footers, cards and contact blocks. Phone (404) 383-4574. Evening and weekend consultations.
