What actually happens

The hour, step by step.

01

Talk first

What you want changed, what you're worried about, what your timeline and budget look like. No chair yet, no instruments.

02

Exam & photos

X-rays, gum health check, and the same high-resolution photography that guides the lab. If veneers aren't right for you, this is where we say so.

03

The plan

Shade direction against your skin in natural light, design approach, number of visits — and the full price in writing. Full-arch is $8,999, same as the website.

04

You decide later

Take the plan home. Financing options are shown if you want them (soft check, no score impact). Nobody follows you to the parking lot.

The biggest fear

"What if I hate them?"

You will never wake up to a permanent smile you've never seen. The design is proven twice before porcelain exists: first as a wax-up preview you can hold, then as trial temporaries you wear in real life — work, photos, dinner. Only a design you've approved gets fabricated.

If the trial isn't right, we change the design. That's not a favor; it's the process.
