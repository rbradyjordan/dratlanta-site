Our photography pledge

Why every case looks comparable.

Before/after photos are easy to game: better lighting on the after, a flattering angle, a filter. We photograph every case with the same camera settings, lighting, distance, and pose — before and after — and note how long after treatment the result was taken. Every patient shown signed a specific release for this gallery.
