No surprises

Know everything before you book.

01

Does it hurt?

Preparation is conservative and fully numbed. Most patients describe pressure, not pain — and you leave with temporaries the same day.

02

How long do they last?

With proper care, porcelain veneers typically last 10–15 years. We plan margins and bite so yours age well, and we tell you what shortens their life.

03

What if I hate them?

You won't find out at the end. You approve a wax-up preview and wear trial temporaries first — the design changes until you love it, before any porcelain is made.
