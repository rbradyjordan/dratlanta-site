Money questions

Asked plainly, answered plainly.

Why do other practices hide their prices?

Usually because pricing is decided in the chair, where it's harder to say no. We think you should be able to decide whether veneers fit your life from your couch, at midnight, without talking to anyone. That's why the number is published.

Will my quote change after the consult?

Full-arch is $8,999 — the number you see here. If your case needs additional work first (gum therapy, decay treatment), that's identified at the exam, priced separately in writing, and explained before anything starts. No surprise line items on placement day.

Does applying for financing hurt my credit?

Checking your options uses a soft inquiry, which doesn't affect your credit score. A hard inquiry only happens if you accept a plan, and the lender tells you before that point.

Do you take insurance?

Cosmetic veneers are generally not covered by dental insurance. Where a portion of treatment is restorative (correction cases especially), we'll help you document it for any benefits you may have.
