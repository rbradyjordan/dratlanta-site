## Positioning takeaways
1. Own price transparency ($8,999 + one compliant monthly example) — empty lane.
2. Lead with the named, licensed doctor (beats MINT anonymity + ACCD dilution); pursue AACD membership early.
3. Melanin-rich shade expertise = the wedge; no competitor mentions it.
4. Out-gallery MINT with 100% real patients + "no models, no filters" pledge.
5. Answer cost/pain/longevity plainly — absent at all four.
6. Beat Doctible's 2–3 tap booking.
7. Concentrate reviews on Google; show live count on-site; 300+ in 12–18mo passes MINT/Intown.
8. Position "accredited-quality veneers at an honest, published price" between ACCD (unpriced luxury) and MINT (discount promo). Use Cherry + Sunbit.
