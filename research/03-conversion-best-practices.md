# Conversion & Compliance Best Practices (2025–2026)

## Mobile conversion
- 60–67% of dental traffic is mobile; each extra step costs ~15% of completions; optimized practices convert 8–12% vs 2–4% average.
- Sticky bottom bar: Call (tel:) + Book, on every page. 24/7 self-serve path for late-night researchers. Forms: name/phone/reason first, progressive disclosure (NN/g).
- CWV targets: LCP ≤ 2.5s (aim 2.0), INP ≤ 200ms, CLS < 0.1.

## Financing compliance (Reg Z / TILA §1026.16/.24)
- Stating "$X/mo", # of payments, or term = "triggering term" → ad must clearly disclose APR, repayment terms, down payment.
- Safe: general language ("flexible monthly plans", "0% options for qualified applicants") or the lender's approved representative-example block (Sunbit format: purchase amount, APR, down payment, term, monthly, total).
- "As low as" must be genuinely available to a meaningful share of applicants.
- Lender notes: Cherry (0% qualifying, 6wk–60mo, from 5.99%), Sunbit (8.99–35.99% APR, no late fees/deferred interest), CareCredit (deferred-interest promos = reputational risk).
- Monthly framing measurably lifts case acceptance (Cherry provider data: 25–90%).

## Gallery
- Standardize: same views, lighting, distance, retraction (FTC deception issue otherwise).
- Label: procedure, # veneers, material, timeline, months post-treatment. Diverse ages/ethnicities.
- Slider for veneers (immediate change); side-by-side thumbnails in grid + slider in case detail.
- HIPAA: photos are PHI; signed use-specific releases naming website/social/ads separately; revocable, tracked.

## Content
- Highest-converting asset: "What to Expect at Your Consultation" page + trial-smile/mock-up answering "what if I hate it".
- FAQ answering pain, longevity (10–15 yrs), cost plainly; FAQ schema.

## Local SEO sequence for new brand
1. GBP created/verified with final NAP before anything else.
2. `Dentist` schema mirroring GBP exactly (67% of implementations fail validation — validate).
3. Data aggregators (Data Axle, Neustar Localeze, Foursquare).
4. Claim Healthgrades, WebMD, Vitals, Zocdoc (Zocdoc = highest conversion: reviews + booking together).
5. Update NPPES NPI record — directories seed from it.
- Reviews: post-visit SMS/email asks, rotate platforms, ~50 Google reviews = ranking threshold. NO incentives (FTC 2024 rule), NO gating, HIPAA-safe replies (never confirm patienthood).

## Design system norms
- prefers-reduced-motion (WCAG 2.3.3); crossfades OK; kill parallax/autoplay loops.
- 16px+ body, ~1.2–1.25 scale, WCAG AA 4.5:1 (watch thin gold-on-white).
- AVIF/WebP + srcset, lazy-load below fold, preload LCP hero, explicit dimensions, ≤ ~1MB above-fold.

## Melanin-rich shade positioning
- Real clinical substance (undertone/Fitzpatrick shade selection, BL1/BL2/B1 for deep skin tones, translucency, gingival pigmentation/margin contrast) but nobody owns it as practice positioning. Closest: Dr. Catrise Austin (celebrity/affordability angle, not shade expertise).
- Opportunity: pillar page + skin-tone-diverse gallery = near-unique differentiator.

State caveat: GA dental board advertising rules apply on top of FTC/Reg Z (testimonials, "specialist" claims) — review before launch.
